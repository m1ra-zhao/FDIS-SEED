Get involved

Join the community

Whether you want to lead a project, lend a hand, or simply learn more — there is a place for you in SEED.

Why join

Turn ideas into action

SEED is built around practical, student-led work. As a project member you help plan and carry out real initiatives. As a project leader you shape what those initiatives become.

You don't need to be an expert on sustainability. Curiosity, ideas, and a willingness to help are enough to start.

Ways to take part

Start small

  • Join an existing project as a project member.
  • Propose a new project and lead it.
  • Volunteer at events and campaigns.
  • Help share sustainability ideas with the school community.
