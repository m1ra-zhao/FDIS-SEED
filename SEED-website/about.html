About SEED

A community that grows change

SEED is not simply an environmental awareness club. It is a student-led community that aims to turn sustainability awareness into practical action.

Our mission

What we exist to do

SEED exists to promote sustainability within the school campus, spread awareness and educate school members about sustainable ways of living, and advocate for environmentally beneficial policies and events.

We also develop and coordinate student-led sustainability projects, and collaborate with external organisations that share similar environmental goals.

Our approach

How we work

01

Projects are designed, led, and carried out by students.

02

Sustainability education & awareness

Helping students and staff understand sustainable ways of living.

03

Turning awareness into real, on-campus change.

04

Collaboration

Working with like-minded organisations that share our goals.

05

An inclusive community

A non-discriminatory space open to everyone.

Our organisation

How we are structured

SEED consists of a leading committee, project leaders, and project members. Roles may vary depending on the needs of individual projects.

Most of the club's practical work happens inside individual projects. A project has a clear goal, a project leader (or leaders), and the members who carry the work forward.
